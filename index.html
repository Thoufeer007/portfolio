Sheet 02 · Profile

Architect first. Then delivery lead.

Trained as an architect at SPA New Delhi, with a dual Master's in project management (SPA New Delhi) and global BIM management (Universitat de Barcelona · Zigurat), he moved into BIM and information management: metros, an airport and Saudi giga‑projects at Techture, a €3.2M lab with CBRE, a theme park for Qiddiya.

      Sheet 04 · How he runs delivery

      Six steps, one model.

      The same six steps on a metro, an airport or a precast office. AI can draft; a person always signs.

      1. 01

        Brief

        What the client needs, written as information requirements before anyone models anything.

      2. 02

        Standards

        Naming, containers and checks set once (ISO 19650), so people and scripts follow the same rules.

      3. 03

        One live model

        Drawings and quantities are read from the model, never retyped.

      4. 04

        Automated draft

        Scripts and AI place rebar, views, tags and schedules: the repetitive work.

      5. 05

        Independent check

        A second, blind check that refuses mismatches: clash tests, audits, recounts.

      6. 06

        Sign-off

        Nothing is done until a person checks the evidence and signs, like the 1,360 sheets on Delhi Metro.
